Evidence boundary

Validation and limits

Green checks mean something specific. They do not mean everything imaginable.

On this page

  • What was checked
  • What validation means
  • Current limits

What was checked

  • Canonical SOP, Rupert Giles persona, PNG, and ICO custody by SHA-256. Current LF source and frozen v0.1.0 physical bytes are identified separately where line endings differ.
  • Source, artifact, and revision separation.
  • Physical-byte hash parity for stored artifacts.
  • Relative manifest links and explicit revision parents.
  • Collision refusal and transactional revision publication.
  • UTF-8, timestamps, sigils, and Windows-safe header handling.
  • Rejection of empty scaffolds and summary-shaped outputs.
  • Subtitle-only yt-dlp command construction without cookies, credentials, playlists, or audiovisual download.
  • Codex package structure and a live local Codex installation path.
  • Claude folder and upload-ZIP structure.
  • Windows extraction of the complete customer ZIP and nested Claude ZIP.

What validation means

The deterministic validator checks required structure and selected custody rules. Rupert Giles still judges semantic clustering, source fidelity, bridge quality, sigil intent, and retrieval value.

Current limits

  • Yammerknit does not include embeddings, a vector database, or retrieval execution.
  • Live YouTube acquisition requires a separately installed yt-dlp and accessible subtitles.
  • Audio transcription is outside the helper.
  • Claude installation and activation require a Claude host; package validation alone does not prove live Claude behavior.
  • Public plugin-directory review and publication are separate from the public repository release.
Version scope: these claims apply to Yammerknit 0.1.0 and the evidence published with that release.
Inspect the machinery: open the public verification directory. Propose a change: read the contribution guide.
