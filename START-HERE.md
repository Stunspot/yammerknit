# Start here: turn a source into reusable, inspectable meaning

Yammerknit connects a document's claims without losing their source or limits. Begin with [installation](customer-docs/INSTALLATION.md) for your host, or open [the complete worked example](skills/yammerknit/examples/README.md) to see the source, full artifact and the difference between a faithful extraction and a confident misreading.

After installation, supply your source and ask: “Create a Yammerknit for this material. Preserve qualifiers and source locators; label inference and proposed transfer.” Name a workspace only when you want durable records. A source saved is not yet an artifact created, and an artifact created is not yet an operating retrieval system.

Follow [create, save, browse and return](customer-docs/USE-YAMMERKNIT.md) for the working journey. Use [data and portability](customer-docs/DATA-AND-PRIVACY.md) to move the complete record, [recovery](customer-docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) for a failure, and [validation and limits](customer-docs/VALIDATION-AND-LIMITS.md) to interpret a pass or reproduce the kit.
